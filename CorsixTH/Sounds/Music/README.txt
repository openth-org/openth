OpenTH's own background music: 23 songs written for OpenTH in 2026 in the
style of Theme Hospital's music (none of them uses its melodies). They are
XMI files like the original game's and play the same way.

names.txt gives each file's title, in the order the jukebox lists them.
The game plays them after Theme Hospital's own songs (Lua/audio.lua). A
player who chooses their own music folder (Settings, Folders, Music) hears
only that folder.
